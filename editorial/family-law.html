Family Law

Divorce, custody, support, modification, enforcement and marital agreements. Six practice areas, filed and heard in the Hartford Judicial District.

Connecticut is an all-property state. The court can reach anything you own.

Connecticut is not a community property state, and it has no alimony formula. Under C.G.S. §46b-81 the court may assign any asset either spouse owns — including property brought into the marriage and property inherited during it. Every matter begins with a clear assessment of your position under those rules.

Domestic violence — family violence only.

Family-violence matters only. Connecticut arraigns a family-violence arrest very quickly, and the protective order is usually set at that first appearance. The criminal practice is limited to family-violence matters. The firm does not accept general criminal defense.
