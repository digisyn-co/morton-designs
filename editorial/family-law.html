Practice Area [01]

Family Law

Separation, property, and complex parenting matters handled with strategic precision.

Family matters are decided once. They are lived with for years.

We act for clients whose separations involve significant assets, business interests, or contested parenting arrangements. Every matter begins with a clear assessment of your position and the range of outcomes available to you.

What We Handle
01

Separation and divorce

Advice on timing, disclosure obligations, and the practical steps that follow a decision to separate.

02

Property and financial settlement

Identification and valuation of the asset pool, including trusts, companies, and superannuation.

03

Parenting arrangements

Care schedules, relocation, and disputes where the arrangements cannot be agreed between parents.

04

Consent orders and agreements

Binding financial agreements and consent orders drafted to withstand later challenge.

05

Urgent applications

Injunctions and interim orders where assets, children, or safety are at immediate risk.

06

Intervention orders

Applications and responses, including matters running alongside criminal proceedings.

How A Matter Runs
01
Initial conference

A full picture of the relationship, the assets, and the children before any advice is given.

02
Disclosure and valuation

Financial disclosure is exchanged and the asset pool is established with independent evidence.

03
Negotiation or mediation

Most matters resolve here. We prepare as though they will not.

04
Proceedings

Where agreement is not possible, the matter is run to final hearing with counsel briefed early.
