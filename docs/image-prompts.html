Image Brief

Morton — site-wide image prompts

Prompts for Google Flow. Every image on the site is listed below with its slot name, aspect ratio, and prompt. Paste the style preamble at the start of each prompt, and the negative list into the negative field. Frames marked motion are the only ones intended as video; everything else should be a still.

Style preamble

Cinematic editorial photograph for an elite law firm. Deep midnight-navy palette, near-black shadows, one soft directional light source. High contrast, heavy negative space, shallow depth of field, 85mm lens, muted warm-champagne highlights. Restrained, quiet, expensive. Photorealistic, fine grain, natural colour, no HDR, no saturation boost.

Negative prompt

text, lettering, signage, logos, watermarks, gavels, scales of justice, law books as props, stock-photo handshakes, flags, courtroom seals, crowds, blue-orange grading, lens flare, tilt-shift, fisheye, plastic skin, extra fingers, distorted architecture.

Consistency rules

  • One light direction across the whole set: light enters from the left, shadow falls right.
  • One building. Treat every architectural frame as the same imagined courthouse and chambers — same stone, same timber, same era.
  • Nobody's face appears anywhere except the attorney portraits.
  • Every frame must survive a navy scrim at 25–90% opacity and still read. Shoot dark; do not deliver bright images.
  • Deliver at 2× the listed pixel width for retina.
