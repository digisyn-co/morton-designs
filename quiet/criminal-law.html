04  —  Sequence

01Arrest

Connecticut requires an arrest where the officer finds probable cause that family violence occurred. C.G.S. §46b-38b.

02Arraignment

The next court day, at G.A. 12 in Manchester for a Glastonbury arrest. Family Services interviews you first, and the protective order is set here.

03Protective order

Full, residential or partial. What it restricts — contact, the home, the children — is decided at arraignment.

04Program or plea

Eligibility for the Family Violence Education Program is assessed early. C.G.S. §46b-38c(g).

05Disposition

Dismissal on completion of the program, a negotiated resolution, or trial — decided on the file rather than on instinct.

05  —  Questions
What happens after a family violence arrest in Connecticut?+

Arraignment comes very quickly, and protective-order conditions are typically set at that first appearance.

What is the Family Violence Education Program?+

A diversionary program that can lead to dismissal for eligible cases.

What is the difference between a protective order and a restraining order?+

One issues from the criminal case. The other is a civil order applied for by the protected party.

What is coercive control under Jennifer's Law?+

A pattern of controlling behavior that can support a civil restraining order without physical violence.

How does a family violence case affect custody?+

It bears directly on the best-interest analysis and can shape or restrict the parenting schedule.

Do you handle criminal cases other than domestic violence?+

No. The criminal practice is limited to family-violence matters, and the firm does not accept general criminal defense.
