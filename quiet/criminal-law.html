05  —  Questions
Should I answer police questions?+

Get advice first. What is said in an interview is difficult to undo, and the decision depends on facts that should be reviewed before you attend.

Will this appear on my record?+

It depends on the charge, the outcome, and the jurisdiction. This is assessed early, because it often shapes the whole strategy.

Can I change lawyers mid-matter?+

Yes. The existing file is reviewed and you are told plainly what can still be changed and what cannot.
