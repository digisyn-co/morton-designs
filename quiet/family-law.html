05  —  Questions
Is Connecticut a 50/50 divorce state?+

No. Property is divided equitably, and the court may assign any asset either spouse owns.

Can the court divide property I owned before the marriage?+

Yes. Connecticut is an all-property state, so premarital, inherited and gifted assets can be reached. That is the strongest argument for a marital agreement.

How is alimony calculated in Connecticut?+

There is no formula. The court weighs the statutory factors in C.G.S. §46b-82, including length of marriage, earnings and needs.

Does joint custody mean equal time?+

No. Joint legal custody concerns decision-making. The parenting plan sets the schedule, and the two are decided separately.

Who pays for college after a divorce?+

The court can enter an educational support order under C.G.S. §46b-56c, but it generally must be sought before the child turns eighteen.

What are the automatic orders?+

Court orders binding both spouses from the moment the case starts, restricting transfers of property, travel with children and changes to insurance.
