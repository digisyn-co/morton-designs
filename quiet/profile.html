01  —  Background

Daniel M. Morton has been admitted to practice in Connecticut since April 10, 2017, and founded Morton Family and Criminal Law, PLLC to focus on Connecticut family law — with a limited family-violence defense capability alongside it.

The family practice covers divorce, custody and visitation, child support, modification, enforcement and contempt, and prenuptial and postnuptial agreements. Glastonbury family matters are filed and heard in the Hartford Judicial District at 90 Washington Street.

The criminal side is deliberately narrow: family-violence matters only, arraigned at G.A. 12 on Center Street in Manchester. Running both dockets means seeing how a family violence case and a divorce affect each other, which is the reason the two sit under one roof.
