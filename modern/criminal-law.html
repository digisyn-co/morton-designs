03 / Questions

General information only. It is not advice about your matter.

Should I answer police questions?+

Get advice first. What is said in an interview is difficult to undo, and the decision depends on facts that should be reviewed before you attend.

Will this appear on my record?+

It depends on the charge, the outcome, and the jurisdiction. This is one of the first things we assess, because it often shapes the whole strategy.

Can I change lawyers mid-matter?+

Yes. We review the existing file and tell you plainly what can still be changed and what cannot.
