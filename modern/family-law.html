03 / Sequence

How a
Connecticut
case runs.

01

Consultation

Your position, the assets, the children, and the range of realistic outcomes — before any strategy is set.

02

Filing & automatic orders

The automatic orders under P.B. §25-5 bind both parties from the start of the case. Knowing what they restrict comes first.

03

Financial affidavit & disclosure

Connecticut runs on the sworn financial affidavit. Incomplete disclosure is the most common cause of delay and cost.

04

Family Services & negotiation

Most matters resolve through Family Services mediation or direct negotiation, reviewed by the court under C.G.S. §46b-66.

05

Hearing or judgment

Where agreement is not possible, the matter is heard at 90 Washington Street in Hartford.

04 / Questions

Asked
every week.

General information only. It is not advice about your matter.

All Questions →
Is Connecticut a 50/50 divorce state?+

No. Property is divided equitably, and the court may assign any asset either spouse owns.

Can the court divide property I owned before the marriage?+

Yes. Connecticut is an all-property state, so premarital, inherited and gifted assets can be reached. That is the single strongest argument for a marital agreement.

How is alimony calculated in Connecticut?+

There is no formula. The court weighs the statutory factors in C.G.S. §46b-82, including length of marriage, earnings and needs.

Does joint custody mean equal time?+

No. Joint legal custody concerns decision-making. The parenting plan sets the schedule, and the two are decided separately.

Who pays for college after a divorce?+

The court can enter an educational support order under C.G.S. §46b-56c, but it generally must be sought before the child turns eighteen.

What are the automatic orders?+

Court orders binding both spouses from the moment the case starts, restricting transfers of property, travel with children and changes to insurance.
