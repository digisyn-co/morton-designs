A full picture of the relationship, the assets, and the children before any advice is given.

03 / Questions

Asked
every week.

General information only. It is not advice about your matter.

Do we have to go to court?+

Most matters settle without a final hearing. Court is one option among several, and the decision to file is a strategic one made with you rather than a default.

How long does a property settlement take?+

It depends on the complexity of the asset pool and whether disclosure is provided cooperatively. We give an estimated range at the first conference and revise it as the matter develops.

What will it cost?+

[Fee structure to be supplied.] You receive a written costs agreement before work begins, and an update whenever the estimate changes.

Can you act if my matter is already on foot?+

Yes. Taking over a matter mid-stream is common. We review the file, identify what has been missed, and tell you plainly what can still be changed.

Is the first conversation confidential?+

Yes, and it carries no obligation to proceed.
